# Antenna probe
_The probe for the horn antenna that connects to a coaxial cable output_

Last updated: 2026-07-19

The probe is made from a piece of round (assumed aluminium) pipe, approximately 1209mm long. 
The ends are not cut perfectly flush. The diameter (outer to outer) is about 49mm. The metal 
is about 2mm thickness. The probe length should be reduced to about 1000mm for better 
performance, as initial tests show that it is nopt 

The far end (inserted deepest into the waveguide) is covered in copper tape that is intended 
to prevent common mode currents from flowing on the inner surface of the pipe.

The near end has 7-strand copper Earth cable attached with roughly equal spacing all the way 
around the pipe circumference using copper tape. Roughly 3-4cm of the wire strands overlaps 
the outer surface of the pipe. Each strand is attached by a small piece of copper tape, and 
then a wrap of copper tape goes around the full circumference, over the strands and smaller 
pieces of tape. The end of the pipe was not otherwise covered, so is open to the air.

The wire strands then taper to a standard Earth wire over a roughly 10cm length. Within the 
wire the strands are twisted, and covered in the normal insulating sleeve. This then 
connects to the centre pin of an SMA connector that is attached to a custom-made mesh ground 
plate that can be bolted to the mesh, with washers and nuts compressing it against the mesh 
to make a good electrical contact. The centre pin connection has not yet been finalised, but 
we will try a simple socket (e.g. banana plug) in the first instance.

The support structure for the probe within the waveguide has not yet been properly designed, 
but is currently made from a long piece of wood with velcro cable ties used to attach the 
aluminium tube to it.

## Copper tape ground plate

For testing, Ahmed and Jordan have fashioned a plate out of copper tape that has an SMA connector for attaching the whip antenna. Wire wool was used to clean the rust off the waveguide mesh and the copper tape plate was then taped to the side in order to secure a good ground contact. This resulted in a very good probe S11, albeit in a narrower band (2026-07-14).
